# Email Testing Inbox Decision Helper

A small helper for webmasters, marketers, QA testers, and developers who need to test email flows without filling their main inbox.

It helps decide when to use:

- a real inbox
- an email alias
- a receive-only temporary inbox

## Useful for testing

- newsletter signup forms
- lead magnet delivery
- contact forms
- onboarding emails
- low-risk transactional email receipt
- SaaS/product evaluation flows

## Basic rule

Use a **real inbox** for anything important or long-term.

Use an **email alias** when you want privacy but still need recovery and long-term access.

Use a **receive-only temporary inbox** only for short-lived, low-risk workflows where losing access does not matter.

For short-lived receive-only inbox workflows, see:

https://smailpro.com/use-cases

## Do not use temporary email for

- billing
- client accounts
- account recovery
- banking
- healthcare
- crypto
- government services
- legal records
- anything where permanent access matters
